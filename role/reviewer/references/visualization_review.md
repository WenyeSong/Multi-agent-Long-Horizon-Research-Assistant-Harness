# Visualization Review

Figures are reviewed only insofar as they support existing claims or explicit
requirements.

## Review Questions

- Was a figure explicitly requested by the task or planner?
- Does the figure support a claim in the text?
- Are axes, units, parameters, or captions clear enough for the claim?
- Is the figure consistent with raw data and reported numbers?

## Severity Guidance

- Missing optional extra plot: advisory
- Caption could be clearer but the figure is understandable: advisory
- Plot labels are poor enough to obscure a core conclusion: major
- Figure contradicts raw data or text: blocking
- Explicitly required plot is absent: major or blocking, depending on importance
